For Christmin

Christmin, my love, this page is for you. You gave up a classroom you had served for twenty-four years, so that you could walk beside me into the world. You did not just come along. You chose this, you chose us, and every mile since has been better because you chose it. You are the reason our trips feel the way they do. You pack with a care no one sees, the warm coat, the snack for the long ride, the little thing that turns a cold morning into a comfortable one. You notice beauty first and point it out, so I look again and see it too. You turn a long journey into a conversation, a wrong turn into a story, and a strange city into a place that feels like home, because you are in it. Thank you for being my wife, my best friend, and my fellow traveller. These drawings are only drawings. The real story is you, beside me, every day of the journey. I love you.
